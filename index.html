youtube-uploader

youtube-uploader is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of a YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API v3 with the single scope youtube.upload to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel from the owner's computer.

The tool is not distributed to the public, has no other users, and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.
